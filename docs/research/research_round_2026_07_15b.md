## Evidence contract

- A mechanism is an executable internal structure, not a word or hand-authored
  rule. It is retained only if it compresses past observations, predicts unseen
  transitions, and improves a frozen fresh world.
- Worlds are generated from raw local dynamics. Their hidden evaluation seeds
  and scoring stay outside policy memory. The agent sees observations and
  permitted aggregate feedback only.
- Baselines are fixed substrate, random mutation, replay-memory lookup, and
  any accidentally supplied named-primitive control. A result is positive only
  if it exceeds all relevant controls at equal total budget.
- All claims require fresh-cache replay, source/CSV/report agreement,
  anti-answer-leak scans, and an honest distinction between negative evidence,
  blocked infrastructure, and incomplete work.
