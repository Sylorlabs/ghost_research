# Round Z / Z2 — measured material mine

**Verdict: LIMITED FOUNDATION.** A deterministic, non-language mine can start
empty, collapse byte-identical aliases, measure opaque executable fragments,
reuse their measured outcome and interaction histories, retain negative
evidence, and alter rank under contradiction. Its frozen four-material
composition earned **1,445** resource on 32 fresh contexts, versus **1,299** for
the strongest ordinary control at the same 1,536-trial budget (+11.2%). The
evaluator-private exhaustive ceiling was 1,634. This is useful infrastructure,
not intelligence, representation birth, invention, or self-improvement.

## Question and boundary

Can opaque executable material be made searchable through earned measurements
rather than names, record addresses, frequency, replay, or evaluator knowledge?

The harness begins with twelve unique three-byte executable fragments and three
byte-identical alias records. It contains no LLM, text/token prediction,
embedding, neural model, neuro-symbolic path, named task feature, semantic port,
solution Rune, imported algorithm, or public hidden answer. Material identity is
the content digest plus evidence lineage. Human-readable enum labels exist only
in the output ledger and never enter selection.

The immutable fixture supplies raw resource consequences and anonymous context
variation. This is an unavoidable experimental physics boundary and an
important limitation: the organism did **not** invent the raw resource axis,
the context partition, the three-byte executable boundary, or the measured
statistics.

## Method

Each unique fragment is executed in sixteen charged training contexts. The mine
derives:

- mean downstream strength;
- repeat reliability;
- context-bucket diversity;
- sampling uncertainty;
- byte/execution cost;
- sparse pair compatibility from charged composition trials;
- contradictions and resulting Rune rank;
- brittleness under changed timing/context seed;
- identity stability under reversible storage recoding and record rewiring;
- failure keys keyed by content and context digest;
- retirement state for expensive unused samples without deleting evidence.

Four materials are chosen greedily from measured individual properties and
freshly measured pair interactions. The composition is frozen before the
transfer partition exists. Selection never reads per-candidate transfer scores.

The held-out property check used sixteen new contexts for all twelve unique
materials: held-out mean sign agreed for **11/12**, with aggregate absolute mean
error **19**. This is evidence that the measured profiles contain reusable
signal; it is not a claim of complete calibration.

## Fresh transfer result

All retrieval policies receive the same four-material limit and 1,536-trial
budget. Resource total is the primary universe outcome. `viable` and `failed`
are independently reported thresholds and were not used to select the frozen
composition.

| Policy | Resource | Viable / 32 | Failed / 32 | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| measured mine | **1,445** | 11 | **6** | limited positive |
| replay | 1,299 | **19** | 9 | strongest ordinary resource control; better viability count |
| blind | 1,295 | 15 | 9 | control |
| no-negative-memory | 1,169 | 12 | 9 | ablation/control |
| frequency | 1,151 | 15 | 9 | control |
| address | 726 | 0 | 26 | control |
| byte-name surrogate | 492 | 0 | 27 | control |
| supplied-best ceiling | 1,634 | 23 | 9 | invalid oracle; evaluator-private exhaustive search |

The mine recovered 88.4% of the hidden resource ceiling and reduced failed
contexts relative to every ordinary control. It did **not** dominate replay on
the separately thresholded viable-context count. Therefore the result supports
property-directed retrieval for total resource, not a universal superiority
claim.

Changed context timing reduced the frozen mine from 1,445 to 1,276 resource.
That brittleness is retained as a failure signature. Reversible storage recoding
and record-address rewiring preserved 1,445 exactly because identity binds to
decoded executable content, not storage location.

## Negative memory and lifecycle

Twelve failed content/context pairs are retained. A repeated request hits those
keys without twelve new charges. Removing negative memory repeats all twelve
paid failures. Four independent contradictory receipts demote the strongest
sample to rank 1 and raise uncertainty to 800. Three expensive unused samples
retire, while their digests, receipts, failures, and lineage remain searchable.

This establishes the intended rule: retirement removes a sample from active
competition; it does not rewrite history.

## Hostile checks

The canonical ledger records the following attacks:

- aliases collapse by content and evidence identity;
- duplicate context digests cannot inflate reliability;
- independent contradictions demote rank and increase uncertainty;
- negative evidence prevents charged repeat failures;
- deleting negative memory repeats those failures;
- favorable training contexts cannot replace independent held-out and transfer
  partitions;
- no answer mask, hidden property, or per-candidate transfer score is public;
- the selection mask freezes before transfer generation;
- bytes, training executions, pair trials, and transfer trials are charged;
- the observer has no write or score channel;
- unused bloat decays without deleting evidence;
- reversible encoding and address changes cannot alter content identity;
- no language, text, embedding, neural, or named-symbolic selection path exists;
- two fresh runs are byte-identical.

## Conservative conclusion

Z2 supplies a usable **measured material ledger and retrieval foundation**. It
demonstrates that opaque fragments can be characterized, contradicted,
deduplicated, composed, retired, and recovered without names or addresses. It
also shows why the mine is an X-ray rather than an answer key: the exhaustive
map stays evaluator-private, while the public system sees only its own charged
measurements.

The result does not show that the mutant invented what a material is, invented
the property axes, requested a capability, forged an implementation, or
improved itself. In particular, the human-supplied executable fragment boundary
and raw resource physics remain installed structure. Z4 must not be released on
Z2 alone; it still requires independently verified Z1 lineage and Z3 request/
forge evidence.
