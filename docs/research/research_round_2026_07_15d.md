# Research Round 2026-07-15d (Round V) — the mutant organism

**Status:** GATED — V1/V2/V3 replayed; V4–V6 blocked by missing causal grounding.

**Goal:** Replace Round U's narrow delayed-cell mutation language with an
organism whose executable body, code/data boundary, topology, development,
senses, actions, and mutation operators can all change. The system remains
non-language and non-LLM: no token prediction, embeddings, neuro-symbolic
wrapper, named task features, human tool menu, or preloaded solution Runes.

## Mutable organism / immutable universe

Everything encoded inside the organism may mutate: storage layout, executable
regions, scheduling, cells, connections, state width, developmental sequence,
sensor recruitment, action composition, reproduction, and mutation operators.
The external sandbox, resource/energy meter, provenance log, hidden evaluator,
and immutable raw observation/action transport may not mutate. These are the
physics that prevent answer access, score rewriting, unlimited-resource wins,
and failed-history deletion.

“Mutation-complete” in this round means every organism-owned encoded component
is reachable by mutation within the VM. It is not a claim that a finite VM has
no inductive bias or can efficiently reach every possible program. The fixed
kernel and instruction effects must be enumerated and attacked through
alternative encodings and independent worlds.

## Earned growth and inheritance

- Durable Rune memory begins empty. An organism or mechanism earns inheritance
  only after independent recurrence, withheld prediction, intervention, freeze,
  and transfer. Birth performance and copied evidence do not earn promotion.
- Sigil scratch contains mutable development; commit freezes organism bytes,
  mutation program, recruited ports, energy cost, and ancestry before hidden
  tests; snapshot records aggregate closure; rollback preserves prior history.
- Lifetime adaptation and generational evolution are separate. Descendants may
  inherit verified executable structures and evidence lineage, never evaluator
  answers, hidden seeds, or per-target scores.
- Growth is transferable capability per energy/bytes/time, not body size,
  survival alone, or novelty alone.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for V1–V3. V1 establishes a
broadly mutable organism substrate and self-mutating mutator mechanics, but its
small transfer advantage is encoding-sensitive and cannot establish
representation birth. V2 isolates the decisive failure: a perfect birth-world
boundary collapses after rewiring, especially actuator remapping, because the
organism identifies ports by address rather than by the consequences of probing
them. V3 supplies a bounded two-timescale ecology but deliberately has no
outcome-based reproductive selection. Therefore V4–V6 remain blocked. The next
accepted mechanism must actively probe anonymous ports, infer stable
intervention-response equivalence classes, and reconstruct senses/actions after
rewiring from consequences alone; those earned grounding procedures—not port
addresses—may then enter Rune inheritance.
