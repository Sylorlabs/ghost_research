# AS6 — Independent real-workbench reduction audit

**Verdict: REDUCTION COMPLETE. AS3 and AS4 do not survive as discovery positives.** This is a local, source-and-cached-replay audit: it makes no network request and does not modify the earlier artifacts.

## What survived

- **AS1 and AS2 remain narrow protocol gates.** Their explicit allowlists, denial fixtures, provenance records, and cached replays are useful plumbing. They are not containment proofs, hostile-candidate tests, browsing, or real-world understanding.
- **AS5 is honestly documented as a valid negative.** Its source explicitly compares the earned prediction to fixed, broad, replay, shuffled, and scrubbed controls and emits `VALID_NEGATIVE` when they tie. No positive web-transfer claim remains.

## What reduced

### AS3 is not a real-artifact discovery positive

The claimed 96-artifact corpus is generated by `relation()` and `target()` inside the same evaluator executable; it does not parse the local artifacts it describes. Candidate action, generated target, controls, scoring, and final verdict all live in that one program. It is therefore an implementation-level deterministic scheduling fixture, not isolated discovery over artifacts.

The purported equal-wall-time comparison also fails: only `learned_provenance` is run with `long=true`; every control is run for one pass. Finally, the reported checkpoint curve (`2304`, `4992`, `9648`) is emitted as fixed text instead of being measured from checkpoint state. AS3 may remain a checkpoint/replay scaffold, but its **foundation-positive discovery and allocation claims are invalid**.

### AS4 has a direct target leak

`candidateInput()` first computes `t = target(a, changed)`, then writes a sentinel into `p[t]`. It passes that sentinel as `q` in the opaque frame. The supposedly earned C child chooses the index whose frame component equals `q`. That is the evaluator target encoded directly in the candidate input.

The changed-encoding replication repeats the same construction after recomputing `t`, so it is a second answer leak, not an independent replication. AS4's **structural-process positive is invalid**. The Bubblewrap boundary does not repair an answer channel placed in stdin by the parent.
