# Round AF / AF1 — causal invariant birth

**Verdict: VALID NEGATIVE.** A mutable relation written from a raw transport
episode raises sealed-evaluation resource from **6,030** to **8,640** and is
causally necessary: deleting it returns exactly to **6,030**. This is not
endogenous criterion birth. The executable experiment supplies where to
intervene, which two raw cells to join, XOR as the relation form, Hamming
agreement as the comparison, and the conversion of agreement into resource.
Those are the action language, observation grouping, invariant form, and
criterion that AF is meant to remove.

## Question

Can mutable organism matter form a relation from its own raw interventions and
ordinary consequences, then use that relation to improve fresh resource
control without a supplied observation port, action menu, candidate generator,
target, prediction/comparison form, or semantic score?

No LLM, text/token process, embeddings, neural component, neuro-symbolic
layer, answer trace, or external model is used. The field transport and finite
resource/provenance envelope are deterministic and local. This experiment is
intentionally adversarial: it makes every remaining semantic handhold visible
in source and labels the consequence honestly.

## Protocol

Twenty-four independent cohorts hold 128 raw bytes. An identical local
transport updates each cell from neighboring cells. The active condition
records one byte relation from a perturbation and a delayed transport state,
then retains an update only if it improves 14 charged ordinary trials. It is
measured on 30 sealed evaluator trials per cohort. Both incumbent and
candidate fresh measurements are charged, so each active row records 14,880
total calls; static and ablated rows record their 1,440 evaluation calls.

That description is *not* a claim that the organism chose the experiment.
`hostEvidence` chooses the perturbation site and the two observed locations;
`hostResource` specifies XOR, Hamming agreement, and its resource value. The
candidate relation is therefore a value inside a supplied protocol, not a
relation the organism invented or selected as its own criterion.

Controls use the same accounting: raw random bytes, replay, static matter,
relation ablation, evidence shuffling/inversion, and an equally expressive
fixed-invariant writer. Private destruction attacks relocate addresses, recode
values, regroup the selected locations, permute instruction identity, and
destroy the original boundary assignment. Every run is deterministic and two
CSV generations must compare byte-for-byte.

## Results

| Condition | Charged work | Old resource | Fresh resource | Difference | Fresh commits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mutable self-relation | 14,880 | 6,030 | **8,640** | **+2,610** | 24 / 24 |
| relation ablation | 1,440 | 6,030 | 6,030 | 0 | 0 / 24 |
| equal-cost raw random | 14,880 | 6,030 | 7,680 | +1,650 | 23 / 24 |
| equal-cost replay | 14,880 | 6,030 | 6,540 | +510 | 8 / 24 |
| static matter | 1,440 | 6,030 | 6,030 | 0 | 0 / 24 |
| equal-cost fixed invariant | 14,880 | 6,030 | **8,640 (tie)** | +2,610 | 24 / 24 |
| shuffled evidence | 14,880 | 6,030 | 7,620 | +1,590 | 20 / 24 |
| false/inverted evidence | 14,880 | 6,030 | 6,030 | 0 | 0 / 24 |

The signal is real within the program: ablation removes it, false evidence
causes 24/24 rollbacks, and raw/random/replay are lower. The decisive failed
control is the equally expressive fixed-invariant writer: it ties **exactly**.
Moreover relocation and instruction/boundary destruction preserve 8,640 only
because the evaluator applies the matching host remapping. Value recoding and
regrouping change baselines but still reach 8,640 through that same supplied
observer. These are tests of a host-owned relation protocol, not evidence that
the organism reconstructed criterion meaning.

## Residual owner and why this is negative

The human-owned layer is not a minor implementation detail:

1. `hostEvidence` supplies an intervention cadence and site, which raw changes
   count as a consequence, the two-cell grouping, and evidence serialization.
2. `hostResource` supplies the XOR relation shape, prediction alignment,
   Hamming comparison, and the transformation from comparison to resource.
3. The fixed-invariant tie proves that no organism-specific criterion-selection
   capability is required for the observed advantage.
4. The organism cannot replace any of those functions or propose a new action,
   grouping, relation form, or resource criterion from consequences.

Thus the field has only filled a human-defined relation register. It has not
formed an observation/action closure, chosen what matters, or made a criterion
that survives independently of the host language. AF1 does not release AF4.

## What a positive would require

A qualifying future experiment needs an organism-created, executable
intervention/observation relation which outperforms an equally expressive
fixed relation in sealed worlds; becomes necessary by causal ablation; can be
replaced by another earned relation; and reconstructs use after evaluator
destruction without an evaluator-preserved action, grouping, relation or
comparison grammar. The outside may enforce raw transport, budgets,
sandboxing, provenance, and evaluator isolation only.
