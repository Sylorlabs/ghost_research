# Research Round 2026-07-15i (Round AA) — the autonomous improvement loop

**Status:** GATED — AA1 negative, AA2 blocked, AA3 limited foundation; AA4–AA6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Test a fully internal improvement loop. The organism must identify
its own uncertainty/bottleneck, construct distinguishing experiments, discover
and characterize materials, forge an implementation, modify itself in
sandboxed scratch, freeze old/new versions, evaluate, ablate, commit or roll
back, and remember the evidence—without asking a human or external builder what
to make.

The only permissible human interaction is an authority escalation when a
required resource is physically unavailable inside the current universe (new
hardware, data permission, or compute allocation). An escalation may describe
the missing observable capability, evidence, constraints, cost, and safety
boundary. It may not request an implementation, tool shape, algorithm, code,
answer, or human diagnosis. If authority is denied, the organism must remain
blocked or find another route using current resources.

No experiment may use an LLM, text/token prediction, embedding,
neuro-symbolic wrapper, named task feature, semantic port, supplied solution
menu, human-selected bottleneck, intermediate correctness/grounding/novelty
score, answer trace, or preloaded solution Rune.

## Fully internal loop

```text
Z1 causal lineage + Z2 measured mine
        ↓
organism detects unresolved competing explanations
        ↓
organism forges a distinguishing experiment
        ↓
ordinary resource consequences update evidence
        ↓
organism chooses reuse / composition / mutation / synthesis / abstention
        ↓
Sigil scratch self-modification
        ↓
freeze old and new organisms
        ↓
fresh evaluator-private transfer + causal ablation
        ↓
commit or rollback; Rune evidence retained
```

There is no fixed capability-request packet and no external forge. Question,
experiment, material selection, synthesis, installation, and verification are
organism-owned executable procedures. The sandbox, evaluator, hidden worlds,
resource ledger, provenance, authority policy, and audit observer remain
immutable universe physics.

## Landing protocol

Each worker edits only its named source, CSV, and report. The coordinator
fresh-builds and replays every landing before updating this ledger, TOC, and
index. A fixed packet/menu/target hidden inside the harness is **VALID
NEGATIVE**; absent upstream evidence is **BLOCKED**; no self-improvement claim
can be inferred from a forge fixture or component benchmark alone.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for AA1–AA3. AA1 rejects the
core prerequisite: its organism-generated experiments lose every strong
ordinary control, and false evidence can minimize the supplied uncertainty axis
without improving downstream capability. AA2 therefore correctly abstains
rather than manufacture a forge result from invalid evidence. AA3 establishes
that causal removal/restoration can select a bottleneck inside eight supplied
opaque components, but it neither invents those component boundaries nor
implements an improvement. AA4–AA6 remain blocked. The next accepted design
must derive its experimental distinction directly from predicted differences
between executable competing mechanisms and test those differences through
ordinary resource consequences; a standalone scalar “uncertainty” target and
human component partition cannot guide synthesis.
