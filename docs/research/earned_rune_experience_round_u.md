# Round U U2 — Earned Rune Experience

**Verdict: PASS for the memory machinery; intelligence is not claimed.** An
empty durable store promotes an opaque four-byte executable mechanism only
after independent recurrence, withheld prediction, controlled intervention,
and frozen fresh-world transfer. The canonical lifecycle and eight adversarial
checks replay byte-identically.

## What was tested

The harness borrows Ghost Engine's evidence-rank vocabulary and Sigil's
scratch/commit/snapshot discipline, but imports no Rune shards, VSA state,
solution mechanisms, task names, or Sigil VM operations. Durable mechanism
records contain only opaque bytes, content-bound ancestry, independent origin
identifiers, evidence gates, contradiction/cost state, and freeze state.

Promotion cannot be purchased by repetition. Evidence aliases share an origin,
so copied events cast zero additional votes. The stages require distinct
origins and distinct empirical obligations: recurrence earns `emerging`, a
withheld prediction earns `pattern`, controlled intervention earns `validated`,
and post-freeze transfer earns `verified`.

## Growth is reversible

A contradiction reopens a verified claim and demotes it for specialization;
three independent contradictions retire the stale generalization. Merging two
verified parents creates a `noise` child with lineage but no inherited evidence;
the child re-earns all stages including transfer. Expensive idle debris is
charged by cost and retired when it has no transferred utility. Thus memory can
grow, specialize, recombine, and shrink rather than accumulating static bytes.

## Adversarial result

All eight attack classes were rejected or safely handled:

1. duplicate/alias evidence;
2. replay rescue;
3. hidden-answer injection (fields absent from schema);
4. resource bloat;
5. contradictory contexts;
6. forged ancestry (digest changes);
7. premature promotion;
8. post-test edits.

The record schema also excludes hidden seeds, target answers, per-target scores,
evaluator state, language/text/LLM fields, and hidden-world details. Transfer
returns only a permitted signed outcome into a new transaction.

## Installed biases and limit

Humans still supplied the rank gates, independence rule, contradiction policy,
cost equation, hash, and opaque byte width. This experiment proves those rules
form a coherent answer-free experience economy. It does **not** show that the
four-byte mechanisms are intelligent, that useful topology was born, or that
rank rules themselves were discovered. U1/U3 and later gated experiments must
provide those claims.
