# Round AE / AE2 — endogenous semantic competition and retirement

**Verdict: VALID NEGATIVE.** Two mutable prediction tapes are built from raw
signed interaction consequences, make incompatible executable predictions,
compete on later probes, and the losing tape is retired.  The selected tape
then gains resource on evaluator-private worlds, and ablation erases the gain.
This is a real, reproducible bounded selection effect.  It is not endogenous
semantic birth: the host supplies the tape atom, the mapping from bit to
prediction, the action scanner, complement hypothesis form, probe schedule,
additive comparison language, and commit/rollback transaction.

## Question and setup

Each of 24 cohorts receives only raw signed consequences from interactions
with 16 opaque physical addresses.  It writes two 16-bit pieces of mutable
matter: `H+` records which observed consequences are nonnegative; `H-` records
their complement.  The tapes recommend different subsequent raw addresses.
Six later interactions are charged to compare the resources generated by those
recommendations; the winner is retained while both bootstrap/losing tapes are
retired on a successful sealed-world commit.  No answer values, named task,
solution trace, LLM, text, embedding, neural component, or external data is
available to the cohort.

The claim is deliberately constrained.  Although the tape contents depend on
earned consequences, the experiment source necessarily contains a host
interpreter for those contents: one bit means a positive prediction and a
linear scan turns a bit into an action.  It also gives the organism exactly the
two complementary semantic alternatives and dictates their comparison.  Those
are organism-level semantics, not exterior enforcement physics, so the round's
acceptance gate is not met.

## Results

| Policy, evaluator-private worlds | Charged raw interactions | Old resource | New resource | Ablated | Commits | Rollbacks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| competing mutable tapes | 4,128 | 197 | **1,197** | 197 | 24/24 | 0 |
| random tapes | 288 | 197 | 512 | 197 | 19/24 | 5 |
| replayed preceding cohort | 288 | 197 | 547 | 197 | 20/24 | 4 |
| static tapes | 288 | 197 | 397 | 197 | 18/24 | 6 |
| equal-cost fixed semantics | 4,128 | 197 | **1,197 (tie)** | 197 | 24/24 | 0 |
| shuffled causal lineage | 4,128 | 197 | 522 | 197 | 20/24 | 4 |
| false causal lineage | 4,128 | 197 | 197 after rollback | 197 | 0/24 | 24 |
| private evaluator oracle | 3,840 | 197 | 1,197 | 197 | 24/24 | 0 |

The private oracle is explicitly invalid and is a ceiling only.  It is never
available to construction.  The selected policy retires 48 tapes (two per
committed cohort).  False lineage prevents every commit; shuffled lineage
destroys most of the advantage.  Those controls establish that the raw
evidence matters.  They cannot establish ownership of the language that
interprets evidence.

## Why this is negative

The equal-cost fixed-semantics control ties the constructed competition exactly:
1,197 resource on the same 4,128 charged interactions.  More fundamentally,
the host preinstalled the very things the experiment must not silently count as
organism-owned: a `u16` component boundary, bit meaning, an address/action
mapping, a complement form for the second hypothesis, six-probe scheduler,
summation comparison, and transaction decision.  The organism selected values
inside a supplied semantic language; it did not construct that language or its
selection rule from a uniform causal medium.

Remaining human-owned layer: raw address/tape segmentation; `1 => predict
positive` interpretation; scan-to-action decoder; `H+`/complement `H-`
candidate grammar; interaction order; score addition/comparison; and commit,
retirement, and rollback semantics.  Consequently AE2 cannot release AE4.

## Reproduce

```bash
mkdir -p /tmp/zig-ae2-cache /tmp/zig-ae2-global
zig build-exe sparse_poly_discovery/semantic_competition_round_ae.zig \
  -femit-bin=/tmp/semantic-competition-ae2 \
  --cache-dir /tmp/zig-ae2-cache --global-cache-dir /tmp/zig-ae2-global
/tmp/semantic-competition-ae2 selftest
/tmp/semantic-competition-ae2 run results/semantic_competition_round_ae.csv
```

Expected output:

```text
round_ae_ae2 selftest PASS verdict=VALID_NEGATIVE deterministic=true selection=true organism_owned=false
```

The self-test creates the CSV twice and requires byte-identical replay; it also
requires fresh gain over random, exact fixed-semantics tie, exact ablation, and
at least one retirement.
