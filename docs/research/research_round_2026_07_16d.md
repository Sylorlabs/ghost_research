# Research Round 2026-07-16d (Round AG) — persistent causal loops

**Status:** GATED — AG1/AG2/AG3 replayed as valid negatives; AG4–AG6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Remove the organism-visible criterion algebra entirely. Organisms are
only mutable configurations in a finite-resource raw interaction medium. They
have no visible observation vector, action API, score, target, relation record,
probe grammar, candidate menu, component boundary, or high-level instruction
language. The only admissible selection is physical persistence and replication
under resource-conserving interaction physics. A criterion-like construction
must therefore appear as a self-maintained causal loop: it must preserve a
constraint, recover after perturbation, make descendants more viable, and be
causally necessary when removed.

The exterior may fix only lower-level deterministic interaction transport,
finite resource conservation/accounting, sandbox/provenance, evaluator-private
fresh media, and external authority/safety. It may not expose labels, task
families, reward/acceptance/fitness values, sensor/action ports, observation
grouping, semantic relation form, mutation/candidate generator, curriculum,
oracle, answer trace, LLM/text/token predictor, embedding, or neuro-symbolic
machinery to organisms.

## Evidence standard

A positive needs a self-constructed persistent loop that outperforms equal
total-material/time random, replay, static, and equally expressive fixed-loop
controls in evaluator-private media; has externally verified causal ablation
and perturb/recovery evidence; transfers across private relocation, recoding,
resegmentation and changed medium dynamics; and is not merely selected by a
host-visible scalar. Any host-defined organism-level boundary, replication
template, loop grammar, or fitness comparison is **VALID NEGATIVE**.

## Landing protocol

Workers edit only their assigned source, CSV, and report. Each result must
fresh-build, selftest, deterministically replay, record material/time charge,
include fixed-loop/equal-material and false/shuffled-lineage controls plus
external causal ablation, name residual human structure, and label
**POSITIVE**, **VALID NEGATIVE**, or **BLOCKED**. The coordinator alone updates
this master, the TOC, and the index.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-16)

Fresh independent coordinator builds, selftests, executions, and byte-identical
CSV comparisons pass for AG1–AG3. The wave exposes useful bounded physical
mechanisms: a configuration can persist and recover; a mutable lineage can
improve resource under rewiring; and a rebuilding process can recover through
a private physics/identity shift with exact ablation and false-lineage failure.

No result shows self-constructed persistent causal loops. AG1 is explicitly
host-seeded and template-repaired, and ties the fixed host loop. AG2's tags,
candidate/revision grammar, viability scalar and reproduction scheduling define
its organism. AG3 exactly ties a fixed loop, while host atoms, decoder,
boundary, signed interpretation, builder and timing define recovery. These are
valid negatives, not failed runs. AG4–AG6 remain blocked.

The next admissible edge is a genuinely generative raw medium whose local laws
do not enumerate organism candidates, loop patterns, repair templates, or
reproduction. The external evaluator may measure conservation and persistence
afterward, but the medium must evolve all particles uniformly. Evidence must
show spontaneous emergence from many unprivileged initial conditions,
counterfactual destruction/recovery, and transfer to private law variants;
fixed physical seeds and repair programs must be disclosed as negative controls.
