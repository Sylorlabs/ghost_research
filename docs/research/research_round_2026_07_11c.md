# Research Round 2026-07-11c (Round G) — remove the human prior choice

**Status:** LIVE — 2/5 experiments complete.

**Premise:** Round F established a compounding, partially autonomous
representability engine: it discovers the ORDER2 family, transfers mechanisms
and priors, and assembles to 58/73 versus a 59/73 hand oracle. It did **not**
establish learned prior inference: F1's proxy battery failed its own validity
and leakage gates; F3 also established that the legacy novelty gate
over-rejects genuine capabilities. Round G tests the remaining claim only after
repairing those instruments.

## Frozen wave contract

- New scoped files only; no unrelated worktree changes.
- Every claim needs a standalone Zig harness, raw CSV, report, reproduction
  commands, fixed seeds/budget/alphabet, and an explicit validity-gate table.
- No per-target family name, answer, fixed-precedence label, or human-selected
  structural prior is available to a learned selector/proposer at inference.
- A failed binary/non-degeneracy, own-prior reachability, label-separation, or
  leakage gate makes the experiment **inconclusive** rather than positive or
  negative.
- Terra role owns gate acceptance and final cross-experiment review. Luna roles
  own corpus, selector, invention, and assembly harnesses. Main session commits
  each landed result and updates this table, `RESEARCH_TOC.md`, and `INDEX.md`.
