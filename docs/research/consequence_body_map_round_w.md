# Round W W3 — Consequence-Grounded Body Map

**Verdict: VALID NEGATIVE, with a bounded grounding signal.**

The local non-language harness asked whether a developing procedure could infer
anonymous effector equivalence from raw intervention consequences, freeze the
procedure rather than an address table, and rebuild after unseen body changes.
It could partially do so, but it did not beat the strongest equal-cost controls.
Therefore this is not representation birth, intelligence, or an earned Rune.

## Contract and implementation

The evaluator privately constructs eight effector slots, twelve raw byte cells,
and functional equivalence classes. The organism receives none of those class
identities. Its only developmental evidence is a byte field after an
organism-selected intervention. The procedure keeps per-slot intervention
statistics and mutates its own four-byte tie-pressure state from observed
surprise. It chooses the next intervention by its current uncertainty, then
freezes before all scored worlds.

Certification compares pairwise equivalence (`same consequence class` versus
`different`) rather than asking for a human semantic label. There are 28 pairs
per body, 24 fresh bodies per condition, seven conditions, and 32 interventions
per body. Conditions cover rewiring, organ replacement, a missing/new organ,
delay, distractors, bijective raw recoding, and actuator remapping.

Equal-cost controls are address lookup, random probes, replayed addresses, fixed
probe order, and an equal-size static schedule. A private supplied map is only a
ceiling. The no-probe arm is the causal ablation. Hidden construction, post-
freeze editing, lineage, bloat, raw recoding, and deterministic replay have
explicit hostile rows in the canonical CSV.

Ghost Engine is used only as read-only experimental discipline: mutable
development corresponds to Sigil scratch, the grounding procedure freezes at
commit, and the CSV is the snapshot. No Ghost shard, Rune, VSA state, text
model, named feature, solution mechanism, or answer trace is imported.

## Result

Across 4,704 fresh pair decisions:

| arm | pair errors | error rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| supplied private map (ceiling) | 0 | 0.00% |
| equal-size static schedule | 478 | 10.16% |
| random probing | 492 | 10.46% |
| mutable consequence-grounded procedure | 514 | 10.93% |
| fixed probe order | 517 | 10.99% |
| address lookup / replay | 1,248 | 26.53% |
| no probing | 4,032 | 85.71% |

The learned procedure substantially beats address identity and the no-probe
ablation, including after raw recoding and actuator remapping. This is evidence
that intervention consequences contain usable grounding information. But the
procedure loses to random probing by 22 errors and the equal-size static
schedule by 36 errors. Its three-error advantage over fixed order is far too
small, and the development logic has not earned superiority over simple
coverage.

## Interpretation

The body map is no longer merely an address table: removing probes destroys the
result, and reusing addresses more than doubles the grounded error. However, the
adaptive probe chooser is not yet discovering a better experiment strategy.
Most of the gain comes from the evaluator-installed regularity that response
activity groups equivalent organs. A static or random schedule can exploit that
regularity at least as well.

W3 therefore cannot unlock inherited grounding. The next causal-body experiment
must make probes discriminate competing internal mechanisms, rather than reward
coverage of every actuator. A candidate developmental change must predict which
specific intervention will reduce ambiguity, and that prediction must beat
random/static allocation after raw encodings and causal response families both
change.

## Installed biases and limitations

- The universe fixes eight effectors, twelve raw byte cells, bounded 32-step
  development, and response families distinguished partly by activity extent.
- The organism may mutate developmental tie pressure, but it cannot grow the
  comparison operation or invent a different evidence representation here.
- Raw recoding is bijective and delay is bounded; this is not arbitrary sensor
  physics, continuous embodiment, hostile hardware, or open-ended development.
- Functional classes are evaluator-created synthetic relations. One local
  family cannot establish general body/world distinction.
- The harness tests body-map reconstruction, not autonomous goals, persistence,
  reproduction, general logic, or intelligence.

## Reproduce

```bash
zig build-exe sparse_poly_discovery/consequence_body_map_round_w.zig \
  --cache-dir /tmp/w3-cache --global-cache-dir /tmp/w3-global \
  -femit-bin=/tmp/consequence-body-map-w3
/tmp/consequence-body-map-w3 selftest
/tmp/consequence-body-map-w3 run results/consequence_body_map_round_w.csv
```

The self-test rebuilds two independent ledgers and requires byte-identical
replay, the procedure-transfer marker, causal ablation, freeze, and hostile
checks.
