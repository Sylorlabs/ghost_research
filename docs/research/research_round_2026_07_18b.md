# Research Round 2026-07-18b (Round AL) — matcher-free causal relevance

**Status:** GATED — AL1/AL2 replayed as valid negatives; AL3 audit gate ready; AL4–AL6 remain blocked.

## Premise

AK2 and AK3 establish that earned causal evidence can value interventions and arbitrate explore versus reuse better than random, replay, shuffled history, and fixed policies. AK1 exposed the remaining missing link: relevance binding tied a fixed generic content matcher exactly. The next mechanism must identify relevance by shared causal response to intervention, not raw equality, address, supplied similarity, or a regime key.

The frozen generic substrate remains explicit: uniform raw transport, bounded generic variation/execution, resource accounting, append-only provenance, sealed pre-registered hidden-world generation, and post-run evaluation. It supplies no task label, target, world-family identity, semantic decoder, similarity/matching function, regime/change-point score, experiment menu, answer trace, intermediate reward/uncertainty, or evaluator feedback. No LLM, text/token prediction, embeddings, neuro-symbolic wrapper, or solution memory is allowed.

## Acceptance standard

A **foundation positive** requires an organism-built causal relevance structure to beat an equal-cost fixed generic matcher, random, replay, shuffled history, and fixed segmentation/routing controls in hidden worlds. It must survive private address/value/representation recoding and changed causal mechanisms; lose gain on relevance ablation; remain answer-scrubbed and provenance-traced; and not encode a world ID, regime key, target, or human similarity rule.
