# Research Round 2026-07-15c (Round U) — the growing apprentice

**Status:** GATED — U1/U2/U3 replayed; U4–U6 remain blocked by the U1 negative.

**Goal:** Remove Round T's human-installed topology advantage and test whether
an initially empty, non-language organism can grow its executable body, earn
causal experience, and remain useful as independently generated worlds grow.
This is local original research: no LLM, token prediction, embeddings,
neuro-symbolic wrapper, named task features, hand-written solution library, or
imported research algorithm.

## Irreducible kernel and bias disclosure

No computational system begins without physical rules. Round U supplies only
raw byte/state storage, copying, mutation, execution, scheduling, observation
and action ports, resource accounting, hashing, and sealed evaluation. Every
experiment must enumerate these installed biases. A positive must survive
worlds generated by structurally different machinery and changed raw encodings;
it cannot rely on a learner/world shared topology.

## Earned experience contract

- Durable Rune memory starts empty. No mechanism, concept, answer, family,
  tool, or certified shard is preloaded.
- Candidate structures live in Sigil-style scratch. They freeze at commit
  before hidden evaluation; the snapshot records exact bytes, ancestry, cost,
  permitted evidence, and verdict.
- Promotion is earned in stages: independent recurrence → withheld prediction
  → controlled intervention → frozen transfer. Repeated copies of one event do
  not count as independent evidence.
- Contradictions can specialize, demote, or retire a Rune. Experience must be
  reversible and resource-bounded; accumulated static debris is not growth.
- Memory stores executable mechanism lineage and causal conditions, never
  hidden seeds, per-target answers, evaluator state, or post-test edits.

## Growth contract

- The organism may add/delete cells and connections, change state width and
  update timing, grow temporal memory, split/merge mechanisms, and alter how it
  proposes future structural mutations.
- Growth is capability gain per cost, not byte count. Fixed topology, random
  growth, equal-size static, replay memory, and task-aligned supplied topology
  are required controls.
- Fresh transfer worlds are generated after mechanism freeze using machinery
  not shared with the learner. Channel permutation and raw re-encoding controls
  test whether the learned structure is causal rather than representationally
  aligned.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for U1–U3. U2 establishes an
earned, reversible, empty-start experience economy; U3 establishes an
independent bounded growing-world ecology. U1, however, rejects the key growth
claim: changing topology beats weak controls but not an equal-size static body,
remains far behind a supplied emulator, and changes behavior under raw

it is an encoding-invariant developmental process whose local structures earn
their own observation/action boundaries rather than inheriting them.
