# Research Round 2026-07-19b (Round AR) — nontrivial world foundry and self-authored worlds

**Status:** LIVE — AR1/AR2/AR3 launched in parallel. AR4–AR6 are gated on their evidence.

## Premise

AQ proves only small synthetic pieces: read-only raw-world protocol, bounded pair instrument growth, long virtual experience in a tiny family, and a no-answer-key process record that still needs AP process isolation. More ticks in the same small fixture would make a faster fixture expert, not an inventor.

Round AR raises the workload and world-complexity bar. It requires **meaningful multi-second computation** over a large procedurally varied world distribution; no sleep, no busy-wait padding, and no result that merely reports a fast toy loop. Each full ledger must record actual wall time, CPU work count, world count, hidden-law diversity, checkpoint curve, and deterministic seed. The goal remains bounded experimental evidence, not a claim that a simulation is the real physical world.

## World contract

- Worlds contain many anonymous interacting components, delayed effects, noise, changing laws, distractors, limited action budget, and held-out families.
- Candidate receives raw observations and acts through a bounded protocol. It does not get world rule text, family ID, target label, score/progress feedback, answer trace, or a preferred invention.
- Candidate may author a **world specification** only as a raw compositional proposal. Evaluator independently instantiates it, rejects trivial copies/answer encoding/degenerate worlds, and tests whether its generated world yields new causal distinctions. World authorship is not real-world creation or scientific novelty.
- Candidate claims/instruments/experience remain provenance-bound. Evaluation stays hidden until commitment and replay.

## Anti-toy requirements

1. Work must come from world generation, propagation, observation, intervention, checkpointing, and held-out evaluation—not sleep or deliberate no-op delay.
2. Reports state actual wall time from the current machine, CPU work counters, world/component/law-family counts, and exact caps.
3. Candidate-facing world surface stays anonymous and raw; human-readable family descriptions remain evaluator-side audit material.
4. Positive results are still models. The next step toward non-synthetic knowledge is a separately approved real-data adapter with provenance and no evaluation overlap, not pretending this is the outside world.

## Landing protocol

Workers edit only their named source, CSV, and report. Fresh build, selftest, two full deterministic ledgers, work-profile receipt, exact cap, and scoped diff check are mandatory. Labels remain **FOUNDATION POSITIVE**, **GATE READY**, **VALID NEGATIVE**, **INCONCLUSIVE**, or **BLOCKED**; no claim may call a generated world a real-world invention or general intelligence.
