# Round Y / Y1 — mutable structural responsibility residue

**Verdict: VALID NEGATIVE.** The evolved residue lineage produced a strong
bounded numerical result on independently seeded, delayed and raw-recoded
post-freeze worlds: 3820/4096 viable ticks and 16/32 reproductive worlds,
beating every required non-oracle control. It nevertheless does **not** establish
earned representation-free responsibility. The VM supplied the decisive credit
axis by recording the raw structural slot that launched an action and later
routing its delayed consequence back through a slot-indexed residue decoder.

This is an experiment result, not an agent or infrastructure failure. It says
that mutable residues can preserve delayed causal signal *once the universe has
already made structural lineage addressable*.

## Experiment

The local Zig harness starts 28 empty, anonymous 24-byte organisms and evolves
them for 36 generations. No Rune, answer trace, task label, semantic feature,
body map, language model, text/token model, embedding, neuro-symbolic component,
imported learner, prediction score, grounding score, novelty score or
intermediate correctness signal is present.

Organism-owned bytes control:

- residue creation frequency and pending-record placement;
- eight independently mutable raw-slot routes plus route stride/offset;
- residue lifetime and decay;
- additive versus averaging combination;
- consequence sign and magnitude response;
- exploration, visit response and residue response scale;
- mutation count and mutation displacement distribution.

All 24 bytes are mutation-reachable and participate in the phenotype or its
mutator. The mutator produced different descendant distributions in all 64
paired audit trials.

The immutable universe supplies only execution, bounded bytes/steps, metabolism,
energy yield, damage, persistence, reproduction threshold, provenance/freeze
digests, delayed event delivery, and evaluator-private world construction. The
organism never sees a scalar fitness or its components. The evaluator retains
lineages using only later viable ticks, reproduction and terminal resource
balance.

## Result

All policies receive the same 32 worlds and 4096-tick budget. Post-freeze worlds
use a seed domain disjoint from evolution, new bodies, independently permuted raw
encodings, and delays of 3–15 ticks.

| Frozen policy | Viable ticks / 4096 | Resource balance | Reproductive worlds / 32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Evolved residue | **3820** | **3181** | **16** |
| No residue | 3668 | 895 | 9 |
| Random residue | 3745 | 567 | 12 |
| Fixed residue | 3660 | 1777 | 12 |
| Equal-size static | 3660 | 1777 | 12 |
| Birth-world replay | 3613 | 381 | 6 |
| Shuffled routes | 3688 | 431 | 11 |
| Deliberately false routes | 3654 | 354 | 7 |

The effect is real inside the supplied residue geometry. Shuffling routes loses
five reproductive worlds; shifting each route deliberately false loses nine.
Replay also loses ten. The evolved arm beats the strongest control by four
reproductive worlds, 75 viable ticks, and 1404 resource units.

## Why this is still negative

The harness recorded `(raw structural slot, birth tick, due tick)` whenever an
action was launched. When ordinary energy/damage consequences arrived, the VM
made that originating slot available to the mutable residue router. The bytes
could mutate *how* responsibility moved, decayed, combined and changed future
action, but they did not invent the underlying correspondence:

```text
structural edit/action slot
    -> VM pending record
    -> delayed resource consequence
    -> same record exposes origin slot
    -> organism-owned residue routing
```

That origin record is exactly the missing credit axis Round Y is supposed to
earn. Calling it an anonymous number does not remove the supplied structure.
The raw recoding test shows that the residue procedure can adapt across changed
world wiring, but recoding does not erase the VM's origin pointer. Therefore the
result cannot unlock Y4 or support intelligence, representation birth, causal
credit invention, or open-ended accumulation.

## Controls and attacks

- **No/random/fixed/replay/equal-size static:** all measured at equal world and
  tick cost.
- **Shuffled and false residues:** both lose decisively, confirming that route
  lineage—not mere extra state—causes the bounded advantage.
- **Post-freeze edit:** all 32 attempted edited evaluations are rejected by the
  frozen genome digest.
- **Evaluator rewrite:** 2304 attempted ledger writes are denied; the organism
  cannot change energy/damage accounting.
- **Evaluator leak:** no private world seed, latent effect table, component
  score, target or desired action enters the policy.
- **Transcript memory:** the replay trace is tested only on disjoint worlds and
  loses.
- **Bloat:** every organism byte is charged; residue state is fixed-capacity.
- **Favorable birth:** training and post-freeze body/seed domains are disjoint.
- **Duplicate evidence:** world identity domains are disjoint; duplicate runs
  cannot earn independent evidence.
- **Raw recoding and delay:** every post-freeze body is permuted and uses fresh
  delayed effects.
- **Deterministic replay:** two complete evolution/evaluation runs are
  byte-identical.

## Consequence for Round Y

Y1 does not unlock dependent experiments. The next useful residue angle must
remove the VM origin pointer. Candidate structures must leave locally available
physical perturbations, and later consequences must reinforce or erase those
perturbations without any external record joining an action to its outcome.
Responsibility would then have to arise from the organism's own persistent
dynamics rather than a supplied eligibility address.
