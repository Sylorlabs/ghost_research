# Research Round 2026-07-15g (Round Y) — earned developmental credit

**Status:** GATED — Y1/Y2/Y3 replayed as valid negatives; Y4–Y6 remain blocked.

**Premise:** Round X made experiment programs mutable and showed that their
bytes causally affect descendants, but late resource outcomes did not identify
which internal changes deserved preservation. Round Y tests new locally
designed mechanisms for assigning delayed responsibility without exposing a
human intermediate objective.

No experiment may import an existing learning algorithm, LLM, text/token model,
embedding, neuro-symbolic wrapper, named task feature, semantic port, supplied
body map, curriculum, grounding/prediction/novelty score, answer trace, or
preloaded Rune. The universe exposes only immutable energy, damage,
persistence, reproduction, compute/byte/time accounting, provenance,
sandboxing, and evaluator privacy.

## Y1 angle — mutable structural responsibility residue

When an organism changes an internal structure, organism-owned bytes may leave
a decaying causal residue linking that structural lineage to later actions and
resource consequences. Residue creation, routing, lifetime, combination,
sign/magnitude update, and mutation are themselves encoded and mutable. The
universe supplies no “correct credit” label. A residue is useful only if the
resulting frozen descendants persist and transfer better later.

## Y2 angle — counterfactual sibling organisms

The organism may spend resources to create sibling developments that share a
birth state and differ in one locally selected structural edit. Both encounter
paired but evaluator-private raw worlds; only their ordinary resource histories
return. The organism may retain the structural difference when its effect
replicates across independently paired contexts. Pair construction, edit
selection, stopping, and consolidation are organism-owned and mutable.

## Y3 angle — causal consolidation under interacting mutations

Useful changes may fail alone, interact, or be masked by other changes. The
organism may freeze structural fragments, recombine them, temporarily remove
them, and compare later resource lineages. A fragment earns a Rune only when
its contribution survives repeated add/remove/recombine counterfactuals and
fresh transfer. Byte size, novelty, survival in one lineage, or correlation
with favorable birth cannot earn credit.

## Ghost evidence discipline

Rune memory begins empty and stores only frozen executable fragments,
responsibility lineage, conditions, contradictions, resource cost, and fresh
transfer closure. Sigil scratch contains mutable residues/siblings/fragments;
commit freezes organism and credit machinery before hidden worlds; snapshot
records aggregate resource outcomes; rollback preserves failed history. Ghost
Engine may be inspected read-only but no solution shard/menu/LLM/VSA path is
imported.

## Landing protocol

Each worker edits only its named source, CSV, and report. The coordinator
fresh-builds and replays every landing before updating this ledger, TOC, and
index. Wins explained by a supplied credit axis, paired-world leak, or installed
decoder are **VALID NEGATIVE**; unavailable prerequisites are **BLOCKED**.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for Y1–Y3. All three show
real causal signal: residues outperform controls, edit ablation destroys
viability, and fragment removal erases resource gains. None establishes
self-born developmental credit because the harness specifies what may receive
credit: originating action slots, eight edit addresses, two-byte fragment
boundaries, and signed history/resource differences. Y4–Y6 therefore remain
blocked. The newly isolated frontier is responsibility-unit birth: the organism
must create and revise its own partitions over raw internal change and raw
world history, then demonstrate that those partitions support reproducible
counterfactual intervention and transfer under resegmentation/re-encoding. A
fixed byte, address, action, edit, or fragment boundary cannot be the credited
unit.
