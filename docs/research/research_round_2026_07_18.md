# Research Round 2026-07-18 (Round AK) — causal experiment allocation

**Status:** PARTIALLY UNLOCKED — AK1 valid negative, AK2/AK3 foundation positives replayed; AK4–AK6 remain gated on relevance ownership.

## Premise

Round AJ established two bounded positives: reusable causal abstraction (AJ2) and answer-scrubbed experience compounding (AJ3). AJ4 then identified the next bottleneck. It generated non-fixed experiments and beat a fixed baseline, but lost to random, replay, and shuffled history. Therefore the problem is not whether causal experience exists; it is whether the organism can allocate limited experiments using that experience better than generic search.

The declared frozen substrate remains: uniform raw transport, bounded generic variation/execution, resource accounting, append-only provenance, sealed pre-registered hidden-world generation, and post-run evaluation. It supplies no task label, target, world-family identity, semantic decoder, experiment menu, candidate/tool library, answer trace, intermediate uncertainty/reward score, or evaluator feedback. No LLM, text/token prediction, embeddings, neuro-symbolic wrapper, or preloaded solution memory is permitted.

## Acceptance standard

A **foundation positive** requires the organism's allocated experiment policy to beat all equal-total-cost random, replay, shuffled-history, and fixed explore/reuse controls in hidden regimes. Its causal records must be relevant to the actual regime, necessary by ablation, answer-scrubbed, provenance-traced, and not reducible to a supplied scalar uncertainty or human experiment menu. It remains a bounded frozen-substrate result, never a full autonomy or hostile containment claim.
