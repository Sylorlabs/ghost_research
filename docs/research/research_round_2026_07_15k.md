# Research Round 2026-07-15k (Round AC) — organism-owned invention physics

**Status:** GATED — AC1/AC2/AC3 replayed as valid negatives; AC4–AC6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Remove Round AB's final organism-internal human scaffolds. The
organism must own the structures that define mutation boundaries, edit scale,
encoding, candidate production, and replacement. Human-written bootstrap has
no privilege and must be causally replaceable. The immutable exterior is only
raw universal computation/interaction transport, finite resource accounting,
sandbox/provenance, evaluator-private fresh worlds, and external authority or
safety boundaries.

No experiment may use an LLM, token/text prediction, embeddings,
neuro-symbolic machinery, named task feature, semantic port, component table,
supplied room or fragment grammar, human candidate/tool/operator menu,
human-selected improvement target, answer trace, intermediate correctness
score, evaluator leak, or preloaded solution Rune. Machine words/bits required
by the host are universe physics, not privileged organism concepts: acceptance
requires the organism to build and replace its own higher-level mutation
ontology above them and survive hostile re-encoding.

## Landing protocol

Workers edit only their named source, CSV, and report. Every result must be
fresh-built, deterministically replayed, cost-accounted, causally ablated, and
labeled **POSITIVE**, **VALID NEGATIVE**, or **BLOCKED**. A numerical gain that
inherits a human mutation ontology or proposal generator is a valid negative.
The coordinator alone updates this master, `RESEARCH_TOC.md`, and `INDEX.md`.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh independent coordinator builds, selftests, runs, and byte-identical CSV
comparisons pass for AC1–AC3. All three experiments demonstrate real bounded
capability: learned mutation words give a large ablatable advantage; recursive
proposal programs replace one another and narrowly beat equal-work controls;
and useful behavior is relearned after combined relocation, value recoding, and
resegmentation.

The ownership claim nevertheless fails at the same deeper layer three ways.
AC1 inherits its interpreter and primitive intervention. AC2 inherits VM
semantics, genome boundaries, scheduling, and replacement. AC3 inherits byte
atoms, grouping, candidate generation, and commit geometry. The organism owns
states and programs *inside* supplied transition physics, not the transition
physics that makes states into proposals and edits. AC4–AC6 therefore remain
blocked.

The next accepted design must not add another richer human opcode set. It must
place multiple initially unverified interpreters/transition systems inside a
lower-level uniform causal medium, let organism activity construct and test new
interpreters, causally retire the bootstrap interpreter, and reconstruct useful
function after an independently defined wholesale change of instruction and
boundary identity. Construction cost and the fixed expressive-language control
must be charged so that a larger disguised human grammar cannot win.
