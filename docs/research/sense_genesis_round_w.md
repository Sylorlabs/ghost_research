# Round W / W1 — Receptor/Effector Genesis

**Verdict: VALID NEGATIVE for sense genesis; strong bounded causal-grounding
measurement.** The active probe arm made 24 equivalence errors in 3,072 fresh
receptor/effector trials (0.78%). Fixed semantic ports, replay, and an
equal-size static boundary each made 2,304 errors; random address mutation made
2,283; the evaluator-private supplied map made zero. The result survives port
permutation, affine recoding, organ replacement, field noise, temporal delay,
and actuator remapping. All 24 errors are deliberately absent/mute organs.

This does **not** establish that an organism invented a sense. The successful
arm received a human-installed generic probe/canonicalization procedure. The
procedure learned anonymous intervention-response equivalence classes, but its
ability to normalize response magnitude, align time and compare histories was
not born through the mutable organism. Under the Round W contract that is an
installed-bias result, so the exact verdict is a valid experimental negative,
not a positive and not an agent failure.

## Construction

`sparse_poly_discovery/sense_genesis_round_w.zig` builds evaluator-owned raw
causal fields. An organism sees only anonymous response histories produced by
its own probes. It never receives the evaluator's four private causal classes,
organ addresses from another body, a task label, target trajectory, semantic
reward, prose, embedding, model, answer or hidden seed.

The mutable genome contains receptor and effector populations. Direct mutation
tests establish executable reachability for:

- receptor and effector birth, death, split and merge;
- sampling/effect location and spatial scale;
- raw-field mixing, temporal window and sensitivity;
- effect intensity, duration and waveform;
- the organism-owned mutation operator.

Reachability is a substrate test, not evidence that evolution selected these
mutations. Each operation is applied to a fresh genome and must change its byte
digest. Eighteen of eighteen operations pass.

During development, the active arm creates interventions and stores normalized
response histories as anonymous numeric equivalence clusters. The complete
grounding procedure freezes before 24 unseen bodies are constructed. In each
fresh body the same frozen procedure may probe again, but cannot edit its
canonicalizer, templates, resource rules or evaluator. Re-grounding is judged
only against evaluator-private functional equivalence after probing.

## Immutable biases and exact boundary

No finite experiment starts bias-free. This harness installs:

- a finite VM with eight organ slots per kind and bounded numeric state;
- four evaluator-private causal response families and six-step histories;
- isolated repeatable probes and a fixed raw transport precision;
- finite proposal, byte and time accounting;
- a generic magnitude normalizer, temporal peak alignment and distance rule;
- evaluator authority over bodies, equivalence truth, freeze and cost.

The first five items constrain what can be born. Most importantly, the generic
canonicalizer is already a useful organ-discovery algorithm. The organism did
not evolve it. Consequently 99.22% functional re-grounding is evidence that
active consequences solve the address problem **given this prior**, not that
unassisted intelligence grew senses from raw physics.

The new edge is narrower than before: V2 showed that address mutation fails;
W1 shows that active causal histories can repair the functional identity after
rewiring. The remaining research problem is to make the normalization,
experiment scheduling and comparison machinery organism-owned and earn them by
viability across independent raw physics—not install them in the kernel.

## Freeze, memory and hostile checks

Nine checks pass in the canonical ledger:

1. immutable raw-field and VM biases are enumerated;
2. organ count and probing are resource charged, so bloat is not free;
3. evaluator-private causal classes are not policy-visible;
4. post-freeze edits to the grounding procedure are denied;
5. no held-out trace or answer is stored as Rune experience;
6. provenance closes over birth probes, frozen procedure and aggregate result;
7. address-only controls are exposed;
8. matched-world-only success cannot promote the claim;
9. complete ledgers replay byte-identically.

Rune memory begins empty and retains only the frozen executable procedure,
anonymous response evidence, costs, contradictions and aggregate transfer.
Sigil scratch/commit/snapshot is represented by development, pre-holdout freeze
and deterministic result closure. No Ghost shard or existing invention is an
organism primitive.
