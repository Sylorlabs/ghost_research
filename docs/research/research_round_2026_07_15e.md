**Status:** GATED — W1/W2/W3 replayed as valid negatives; W4–W6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Remove fixed observation/action ports and assigned tasks. Organisms
must grow receptors and effectors over an evaluator-owned raw environmental
field, conduct their own developmental experiments, and earn stable body/world
distinctions through consequences. No human supplies a sense name, action

model, embedding, neuro-symbolic wrapper, or solution Rune.

## Immutable universe / mutable organism

The universe fixes only resource physics and integrity: bounded energy,
compute, bytes, time, persistence, damage/instability, reproduction cost,
provenance, sandboxing, and evaluator privacy. Those quantities cannot be
rewritten by an organism. Everything organism-owned may mutate: sampling
location/time/scale, raw-field mixing, sensitivity, temporal integration,
receptor birth/split/merge/death, effector location/intensity/duration/waveform,
action composition, body topology, development, memory, reproduction, and
mutation strategy.

No finite substrate is bias-free. Each experiment must enumerate the raw-field
physics and mutation reachability it installs. A result must survive alternative
encodings, field permutations, new bodies, and independently generated worlds;
otherwise it is an installed-bias result, not sense genesis.

## No-instruction development contract

- The organism receives no externally assigned job or target trajectory.
  Development is driven only by immutable consequences: resource acquisition
  and expenditure, maintaining organization, avoiding damage, persistence, and
  reproductive cost under changing worlds.
- Exploration policies and intrinsic preferences are organism-owned and
  mutable. The universe does not directly reward novelty, prediction scores,
  human usefulness, or named behaviors.
- A sense/action identity is earned only when an organism-generated probe
  discovers a repeatable intervention-response relation that survives context,
  field permutation, body rewiring, and post-freeze transfer.
- Rune memory begins empty. It stores frozen executable grounding mechanisms,
  conditions, intervention lineage, costs, contradictions, and transfer—not
  port addresses, hidden fields, evaluator outcomes, or target answers.
- Sigil scratch permits developmental probing; commit freezes body, receptors,
  effectors, drives, and mutation program; hidden tests follow; snapshot records
  aggregate closure and lineage.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for W1–W3. Consequence-based
probing is decisively more useful than address memory or no probing, and mutable
organs plus resource ecology are executable. None of the three experiments,
however, establishes autonomous sense genesis. W1's strong reconstruction uses
a human-installed normalization/alignment/comparison/probe procedure; W2 loses
to a fixed sequential drive and never sustains reproduction; W3 loses to
equal-cost random and static probing. Therefore W4–W6 remain blocked. The next
accepted input must place probe construction, temporal alignment, response
comparison, and experiment scheduling inside the mutable organism, then judge
candidate procedures only by later persistence and cross-body transfer under
resource physics—not by a supplied grounding score during development.
