# M3 — Fresh family-transfer matrix

**Verdict: LIMITED POSITIVE.** A frozen, trace-derived split supports perfect
within-region transfer on a fresh evaluator-owned test split (48/48), while
the opposite public family transfers only 24/48 across regions. The frozen
existing-menu control is 24/48 on that same test split. This is a controlled
representation/transfer measurement, **not** a claim that the policy invented
the families, selected a map without supplied trace signals, or beat an
existing menu through a closed loop.

## Protocol

The evaluator owns four opaque tokens, two deterministic public trace regions,
and separate train/test inputs. The policy sees only an opaque token and a
two-number public trace diagnostic. It evaluates the two already-public
candidate families on eight train examples per target and freezes its choice
before the evaluator scores 12 disjoint test examples. The CSV never emits
examples, labels, formulas, target-family names, or private target parameters.

The ledger has exactly one row for every charged diagnostic, train query,
baseline padding call, baseline train call, fresh test call, and cross-region
test call. The candidate arm costs 28 charged calls per target (16 train + 12
test); the frozen existing-menu control also costs 28 (8 individual padding +
8 train + 12 test). Padding is explicitly recorded as individual rows.

## Results

| Measurement | Fresh-test result |
|---|---:|
| Selected family within its trace-defined region | 48/48 |
| Same frozen family across the other region | 24/48 |
| Existing frozen menu, equal cost | 24/48 |

All train/test inputs are deterministic and disjoint (`16+seed..` versus
`128+seed..` bands); target tokens and all evaluator examples are unique.
`selftest`
runs normal and reversed token-order schedules and checks the identical sealed
outcome totals (within 48/48, cross 24/48), rejecting a duplicate/order-based
interpretation.

## Interpretation and limit

This supports the narrow M3 claim: a **pre-existing public family** can have
region-specific transfer that survives a fresh test split, and an apparent
blank should not be treated as one family. It does **not** solve M4/M5: the
diagnostic split is supplied by this controlled evaluator, the public families
were not generated by the policy, and no result compares a trace-derived
proposal system against fixed coverage on a novel sealed suite.
