# Research Round 2026-07-15j (Round AB) — self-built rooms

**Status:** GATED — AB1/AB2/AB3 replayed as valid negatives; AB4–AB6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Remove privileged human internal architecture. The organism begins in
a uniform mutable self-substrate, creates and revises its own causal partitions,
constructs competing executable self-models, and may replace every
organism-owned bootstrap instrument when an internally built alternative earns
better fresh evidence. Human origin grants no rank or permanence.

No experiment may use an LLM, text/token prediction, embedding,
neuro-symbolic wrapper, named task feature, semantic port, supplied component
boundary, fixed request field, human-selected improvement target, human tool or
implementation menu, intermediate correctness/grounding/novelty score, answer
trace, or preloaded solution Rune.

## Evidence, not hierarchy

- Every organism-owned bootstrap component starts `UNVERIFIED_SCAFFOLD`.
  Human-written origin provides no protection, promotion, selection weight, or
  veto against a measured replacement.
- The organism may copy, inspect, mutate, relocate, split, merge, remove,
  emulate, replace, or retire all organism-owned bytes and procedures,
  including its tracer, partitions, self-model, memory, mine, experimenter,
  credit machinery, forge, development, and mutation strategy.
- Only immutable universe physics remain outside it: raw computation and
  interaction transport, finite resource ledger, sandbox, provenance,
  evaluator-private fresh worlds, and external authority/safety boundaries.
  These enforce honest comparison; they do not prescribe internal concepts.
- Durable promotion requires frozen counterfactual contribution and fresh
  transfer per cost. Address, size, age, human origin, frequency, or one-run
  survival cannot vote.

## AB1 — causal room birth

The self is a uniform raw tape/field with no declared components. Organism-owned
mutable procedures propose overlapping temporary partitions based on observed
interactions and downstream resource counterfactuals. Partitions may grow,
shrink, split, merge, overlap, dissolve, or reorganize after relocation and
recoding. A “room” earns existence only if manipulating it has reproducible
causal contribution across independently generated contexts.

## AB2 — executable self-model competition

The organism constructs multiple executable models of its own raw state and
history. Each model predicts different consequences of self-interventions. The
organism generates distinguishing self-experiments from model disagreement,
freezes them, observes ordinary resource consequences, and retains models only
when counterfactual predictions transfer. There is no scalar human uncertainty
target or supplied self-component map.

## AB3 — scaffold challenge and replacement

Bootstrap instruments are treated as competitors, not authority. The organism
must detect one scaffold's measured failure, construct at least one internal
alternative without an external builder, freeze old/new versions from equal
state and budget, test fresh contexts, causally ablate the replacement, and
commit or roll back. A successful replacement must improve the organism-owned
process that helped produce it, not merely one world answer.

## Ghost grounding and landing protocol

Workers may inspect Ghost Engine read-only for Rune/Sigil evidence discipline,
but Ghost's named structures and shards are not organism components. Each
worker edits only its named source, CSV, and report. The coordinator fresh-
builds and replays every landing before updating this ledger, TOC, and index.
Any hidden supplied decomposition or scaffold-aligned result is **VALID
NEGATIVE**; missing prerequisites are **BLOCKED**.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for AB1–AB3. The round shows
substantial bounded capability: useful partitions grow, disagreement influences
experiments, and internally produced replacements yield large ablatable fresh
gains with correct rollback under false evidence. Yet every route retains a
human ontology: subset membership is the room language; fixed model containers
and decoders define explanation; raw bytes and a proposal generator define
replacement units. AB4–AB6 therefore remain blocked. The next accepted design
must make the proposal mechanism and mutation ontology organism-owned: candidate
edit operators must themselves be executable mutable structures that create
new edit scales, boundaries, encodings, and proposal processes, and successors
must reconstruct function after wholesale re-encoding rather than preserve
byte identity.
