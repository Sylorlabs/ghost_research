# Research Round 2026-07-15f (Round X) — experiment birth

**Status:** GATED — X1/X2/X3 replayed as valid negatives; X4–X6 remain blocked.

**Premise:** Round W proved that consequences contain enough information to
reconstruct senses/actions, but the useful probe, normalization, temporal
alignment, comparison, and scheduling procedures remained human-installed or
lost to static/random controls. Round X tests three new local angles for making
the *experiment process itself* organism-owned.

No experiment may use an LLM, text/token model, embedding, neuro-symbolic
wrapper, named task feature, semantic port, supplied body map, human curriculum,
grounding score during development, or preloaded solution Rune. The immutable
universe retains only raw-field transport, energy/damage/persistence/
reproduction physics, compute/byte/time accounting, provenance, sandboxing,
and evaluator privacy.

## Angle 1 — executable experiment organisms

Probe construction, sequencing, temporal windows, normalization, response
compression, comparison, stop conditions, and future mutation rules are raw
organism-owned bytes. The fixed VM effects and reachability biases must be
enumerated. Experiments earn persistence only through later frozen cross-body
viability, never an exposed “correct grounding” label.

## Angle 2 — temporal/causal binding without supplied alignment

The organism receives asynchronous raw fields and delayed consequences. It may
mutate internal clocks, buffers, event boundaries, lag hypotheses, repeated
interventions, and counter-intervention schedules. No canonical time alignment
or distance function is provided to policy code. A useful binder must rebuild
after delay distributions, encodings, and bodies change.

## Angle 3 — ecology judges experiments indirectly

Experiment-makers reproduce only through descendants' later persistence,
resource acquisition, damage avoidance, and reproductive viability in unseen
bodies/worlds. The ecology never exposes body-map accuracy, novelty, prediction
score, human usefulness, or target behavior. This tests whether effective
experiment procedures can emerge from downstream consequences rather than a
human-designed intermediate objective.

## Earned evidence and Ghost grounding

Rune memory begins empty. A candidate experiment procedure stays Sigil scratch
until its exact bytes freeze; only repeated independent downstream transfer can
promote it. Addresses, probe transcripts, hidden mappings, seeds, per-target
scores, and evaluator state cannot be inherited. Workers may inspect Ghost
Engine read-only for Rune/Sigil discipline but may not import its solutions,
menus, shards, LLM/VSA paths, or mutate Ghost files.

## Landing protocol

Each worker edits only its named source, CSV, and report. The coordinator
fresh-builds and replays every landing before updating this master ledger, TOC,
and index. A matched-world or installed-protocol win is **VALID NEGATIVE**; an
unavailable prerequisite is **BLOCKED**; no dependency launches from agent
completion alone.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for X1–X3. Organism-owned
experiment bytes are fully mutable and causally affect downstream viability,
but none of the three angles establishes experiment birth. X1's selection
landscape is flat and decoder-biased; X2 inherits an ordered-lag and byte-change
form that collapses under recoding; X3 evolves a useful lineage but loses a
small fixed experimenter. X4–X6 therefore remain blocked. The newly isolated
frontier is developmental credit: consequences arrive too late and coarsely to
assign credit to the internal structural changes that made an experiment
useful. The next accepted design must create organism-internal, mutable
eligibility traces or structural counterfactuals whose validity is determined
only by later resource outcomes, while preventing those traces from becoming a
human-shaped intermediate reward.
