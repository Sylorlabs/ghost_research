# Ghost Scientist vertical slice — closure protocol

**Status:** COMPLETE — controlled bounded positive for generic program
construction, causal promotion, fixed-portfolio expansion, and held-out reuse.
Autonomous exact-one-tool selection, primitive invention, and hostile-process
containment remain open.

## Why this round exists

Round AX closed the bare-scalar measurement bug, but it did not close the
autonomy claim. Its 27 identities were externally divided among three supplied
tool forms (`source`, `csv`, and `control_flow`), and the staged manifest
revealed the task kind. The resulting 36/108 accepted typed witnesses prove
coverage and payload binding, not 27 inventions, autonomous routing, or an
advantage over a complete fixed portfolio.

This round tests one bounded non-LLM alternative to "make a larger language
model": learn executable measurement programs from intervention outcomes, keep
only answer-scrubbed causal experience, and reuse the earned program on
different real repository bytes.

## Research-record audit

This design was not inferred from one experiment. At closure time the
repository contained 568 Markdown files; `RESEARCH_TOC.md` indexed 418 reports
across 62 numbered rounds. The audit used that index to locate and then inspect
the load-bearing actual reports and source, rather than claiming a line-by-line
read of every document.

The synthesis came from `CLOSURE_PRINCIPLE.md`, `RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md`,
`STRATEGIC_PLAN.md`, `results/NEGATIVE_RESULTS_LEDGER.md`, the representation
and feature-discovery reports, Round P's answer-free forge, AJ/AK causal reuse
and intervention allocation, AP containment, and the AT–AX workshop,
real-artifact, witness, and integration artifacts. The resulting design rule
is: representation is the first gate; evaluator feedback may select only a
precommitted candidate; reusable experience must be answer-scrubbed; a fixed
broad portfolio remains the main competitor; and no result survives without
typed evidence, mutations, ablations, equal accounting, and replay.

## Candidate boundary

The candidate may receive only:

```text
GHOST_CANDIDATE_V1 <opaque_id> <develop|holdout> <payload_hash>
                   <actions_left> <builds_left> <probes_left>
<payload bytes>
```

During development it may receive an opaque accept/reject receipt for a
precommitted probe. It may not receive a task kind, family label, natural
language contract, original path, expected answer, canonical witness, score,
progress total, evaluator source, or a stable development-to-heldout answer
join key.

The candidate output is:

```text
GHOST_WITNESS_V1 <opaque_id> <tool_hash> <program_hash> <payload_hash>
                 <schema_hash> <builds> <actions> <probes>
                 <summary> <canonical_evidence>
```

The evaluator independently reconstructs the canonical evidence from its
private task specification and the exact payload bytes. A scalar, wrong schema,
wrong payload hash, malformed witness, renamed fixed tool, or extra claim
fails closed.

## What counts as construction

`PROGRAM_V1` is a small generic byte-analysis language. Its primitives may
scan bytes, identify delimiters and records, select positions, and construct
bounded spans. It must not contain named AX task alternatives or a
task-to-program compatibility table. A discovered program is identified by
its canonical encoding and hash, not by a worker name.

This is program construction inside a supplied primitive interpreter. It is
not primitive invention, unconstrained grammar birth, semantic understanding,
or open-ended science.

## Real-artifact and control requirements

- Development and held-out payloads are copies of distinct tracked repository
  files. Original paths and evaluator data stay outside candidate input.
- The strong fixed control is the complete three-tool AX portfolio, not one
  deliberately weak member. Matched random-constructor, no-memory, no-probe,
  replay, and fresh-rebuild controls receive the same allowed inputs and
  literal resource budgets.
- Held-out programs and policy state are committed before held-out scoring.
- Reuse counts only when an accepted program earned on development transfers
  to different held-out bytes and costs fewer active construction actions than
  a fresh rebuild. Reserved/charged caps remain equal across arms.
- A positive must be a strict held-out win or a Pareto win at equal accepted
  coverage and lower charged cost. A tie is a negative.

## Unified production-style gate

One coordinator fresh-builds the constructor and evaluator, stages sealed
artifacts, runs development interventions, freezes the answer-scrubbed memory,
runs every held-out arm, reduces typed witnesses, and then executes:

1. equal-budget recount;
2. candidate-input privacy scan and hostile answer/path/score requests;
3. exact payload/tool/program/schema hash binding;
4. relevant mutation, which must invalidate the old witness;
5. irrelevant mutation, which must preserve the canonical property while
   changing the payload hash;
6. no-memory, no-probe, fresh-build, random, and complete fixed-portfolio
   controls;
7. byte-identical replay from a fresh cache.

The published ledger must contain every charged build, action, and probe plus
aggregate closure rows. It must not contain candidate-visible expected values
or post-hoc hand scoring.

## Fresh closure result

The final battery contains nine distinct Git-tracked artifacts: three
development and six held-out. The inherited AX properties are declaration
positions, CSV width deviations, sleep-in-loop spans, and `MissingArg` line
spans. The newly constructed property is deliberately byte-level: positions
of the byte motif `try` outside `//` line comments. It is not a semantic Zig
token claim.

From exact tracked `boundary_crossing/verified_generation.zig`, Luna
precommitted 2,331 payload-derived generic programs. Opaque development
receipts first accepted program 101, after which relevant and irrelevant
mutation checks promoted:

```text
PROGRAM_V1 scan_not_after_in_line 747279 2f2f
program_hash=1bee4f5954df10e4
tool_hash=d09e1e124efc1a7d
```

The program transferred with zero new builds to four distinct held-out
`boundary_crossing` files. A standalone fresh/no-memory construction first
reached it at program 884 on `parametric_guide.zig`; the earned replay used one
action/probe. In the integrated battery every arm reserved exactly 2,048
builds, actions, and probes per item. Active work is reported separately.

| Arm | Total | Develop | Held-out | Active builds/actions/probes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| complete AX fixed portfolio | 4/9 | 2/3 | 2/6 | 27 / 36 / 36 |
| broad fixed AX | 4/9 | 2/3 | 2/6 | 27 / 36 / 36 |

No-memory performs the same development construction and then discards the
program. No-probe precommits the same candidate sequence but cannot promote a

promote. All fall back to the fixed portfolio's 2/6 held-out coverage.

The 671-row final ledger contains 307 per-constructor-attempt rows, ten denied
answer/task/score/path/network requests, nine relevant and nine irrelevant

## Claim discipline

This establishes bounded autonomous program construction, causal promotion,
portfolio expansion, and tool reuse on this designed artifact battery. It does
not establish a generally autonomous scientist, prospective performance on an
external battery, autonomous exclusive tool routing, semantic code
understanding, new mathematical laws, hostile-sandbox isolation, or invention
of new primitive semantics. The grammar, evaluator, task property, and battery
were supplied by humans. The next hard gate is to freeze this constructor
before a separately authored prospective real-artifact battery and run the
exact pair through AP's process-containment boundary.
