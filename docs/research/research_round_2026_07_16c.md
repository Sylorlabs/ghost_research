# Research Round 2026-07-16c (Round AF) — endogenous criterion birth

**Status:** GATED — AF1/AF2/AF3 replayed as valid negatives; AF4–AF6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Remove the last semantic handhold: a host-decided observation or
comparison criterion. Organisms inhabit an unstructured raw interaction field.
They must generate interventions, retain only their own causal consequences,
construct a mutable invariant/criterion, and use it to control ordinary
resource survival. A criterion is not a supplied loss, prediction slot,
feature, target, novelty score, probe grammar, or answer format. It must be an
organism-built relation whose removal makes its earned control advantage vanish
and whose replacement can improve future survival.

Only exterior enforcement physics are fixed: deterministic raw transport,
finite resource ledger, sandbox/provenance, evaluator-private fresh worlds,
and external authority/safety. The host may not supply semantic ports,
observation fields, state grouping, action menu, task family, target,
criterion/invariant/prediction/comparison language, candidate generator,
curriculum, answer trace, oracle, LLM/text/token system, embedding, or
neuro-symbolic machinery.

## Evidence standard

A positive requires an organism-generated relation that: (1) arises from its
own interventions and raw consequences; (2) produces a sealed fresh-world
resource advantage per total cost over raw/random/replay/static and an equally
expressive fixed-invariant control; (3) is causally necessary by ablation;
(4) can be replaced/retired by a newly earned relation; and (5) reconstructs
after private destruction of address, value, grouping, instruction, and
boundary identity. A host metric hidden inside environment code makes the
result **VALID NEGATIVE**.

## Landing protocol

Each worker edits only its source, CSV, and report. Every landing fresh-builds,
selftests, deterministically replays, charges all work, runs ablation and
false/shuffled-evidence attacks, identifies remaining human ownership, and uses
the verdicts **POSITIVE**, **VALID NEGATIVE**, or **BLOCKED**. The coordinator
alone settles the master, TOC, and index.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-16)

Fresh independent coordinator builds, selftests, executions, and byte-identical
CSV comparisons pass for AF1–AF3. The three results are genuine causal partials:
a relation improves resource with exact ablation; an intervention closure helps
under rewiring; and an evidence-dependent successor replaces bootstrap
relations with correct false-evidence rollback.

They are not endogenous criterion birth. AF1 is exactly tied by a fixed
invariant. AF2 loses to a fixed equal-size closure and nearly ties raw/random.
AF3 is exactly tied by a fixed criterion despite strong retirement mechanics.
All rely on host-defined intervention, grouping, relation form, comparison and
resource conversion/acceptance. The organism fills or selects relation records
inside a supplied criterion algebra. AF4–AF6 remain blocked.

The next edge is not another relation-register experiment. It must move the
organism into a raw physical interaction medium where no host observation/
action split or scalar acceptance is available to organism code. The only
selection should be physical persistence under finite resources. A candidate
criterion must emerge as a persistent self-maintained causal loop, be
perturbed/ablated externally, and generalize to private changes of medium
physics. Fixed-loop controls must be equally expressive and construction cost
must be charged.
