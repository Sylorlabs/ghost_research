# Research Round 2026-07-15h (Round Z) — the causal mine

**Status:** GATED — Z1/Z2 foundations replayed; Z3 is a valid negative; Z4–Z6 remain blocked.

**Goal:** Build and experimentally test a fast, traceable local discovery
infrastructure for the non-language mutant organism. It must preserve exact
lineage, measured material properties, negative results, open capability gaps,
and autonomous forge attempts without turning monitoring into an answer key or
letting the organism rewrite evaluator/resource/provenance physics.

No experiment may use an LLM, text/token prediction, embeddings,
neuro-symbolic integration, named task features, semantic ports, hidden answers,
human-selected solution tools, or preloaded solution Runes. Ghost Engine may be
inspected read-only for Rune/Sigil discipline; no Ghost solution shard/menu or
old LLM/VSA path is imported.

## Z1 — causal X-ray

Every structural mutation, developed mechanism, internal activation,
probe/action, raw-world change, delayed resource outcome, freeze, replay,
removal, restoration, sibling trial, and transfer receives a content-addressed
event record. The X-ray guarantees provenance and temporal lineage; it does not
grant causal credit. Causal confidence must derive from explicit interventions
and independently repeated counterfactuals. Evaluator-private fields and hidden
answers cannot enter the public graph.

## Z2 — material mine

Every executable fragment/mechanism becomes an opaque content-addressed sample
with measured strength, reliability, context diversity, compatibility,
brittleness, energy/byte/time cost, counterfactual evidence, transfer distance,
failure signatures, uncertainty, contradictions, and Rune rank. Aliases and
duplicate histories collapse by content/evidence identity. Negative materials
remain searchable so failed work is not repeated. Human names do not determine
admission or retrieval.

## Z3 — capability request and autonomous forge

The organism may emit a machine-readable request derived from observed failure
lineage: missing behavioral capability, constraints, supporting experiments,
uncertainty, budget, forbidden authority, and sealed acceptance/ablation tests.
The forge searches existing samples, composes compatible materials, mutates or
synthesizes implementations, and tests them in Sigil scratch. It commits only
after freeze, fresh transfer, causal ablation, cost accounting, and hostile
audit. Human involvement is limited to granting new external authority such as
hardware/data/compute access; humans do not pick the implementation.

## Immutable boundary

The organism may mutate its body, sensing, action, experiment, credit,
retrieval, request, forge, and self-improvement procedures. It may not mutate
the sandbox, evaluator, hidden worlds, resource ledger, provenance history,
authority policy, or audit observer. Monitoring is read-only and cannot inject
selection scores.

## Landing protocol

Each worker edits only its named source, CSV, and report. The coordinator
fresh-builds and replays every landing before updating this master ledger, TOC,
and index. Traceability is not causality, retrieval is not invention, and a
successful fixture forge is not self-improvement. Installed-answer wins are
**VALID NEGATIVE**; missing prerequisites are **BLOCKED**.

## Coordinator verdict (2026-07-15)

Fresh-cache coordinator builds and selftests pass for Z1–Z3 (Z3 uses its
documented `selftest` and `run <csv>` command forms). Z1 establishes reliable
traceability with intervention-earned confidence; Z2 establishes a useful
measured/negative-aware material repository. Neither is intelligence or
self-improvement. Z3 rejects the self-improvement gate: its request-guided forge
loses request-free and existing-only controls, and removing the forged
structure improves outcomes. Therefore Z4–Z6 remain blocked. The next accepted
forge must consume Z1 intervention lineage and Z2 measured uncertainty directly
through executable distinguishing experiments, without a fixed request-field
packet or implementation fragment geometry; only after the experiment reduces
uncertainty and transfers may synthesis receive additional budget.
