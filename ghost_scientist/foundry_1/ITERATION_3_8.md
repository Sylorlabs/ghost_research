# Foundry-3.8 EggMind artifact and adapted-boundary audit

The highest-priority missing control was investigated before any validation-v6
source, runtime, graph, or outcome was opened.

The EggMind paper calls the framework open-source, but its arXiv metadata and
HTML contain no implementation repository. Six independent artifact searches
over the title, system name, DSL name, and authors found zero matching official
repositories. The source-faithful route is therefore unavailable as of the
recorded search date; this is not treated as conformance.

A narrower `ADAPTED` boundary was built only from explicitly published
material. Two separate strict parsers agree on the canonical EqSatL artifact,
lower the paper-shaped schedule to 61 actions, reject seven hostile mutations,
recompute the paper-compatible motif ordering, and independently reproduce the
published dependency-risk equation.

This establishes an inspectable grammar and scoring boundary. It does not
implement the authors' LLM workflow, proof extraction, egglog lowering, or
end-to-end synthesis, and it does not satisfy A8 or P1.

**Verdict:** `FOUNDRY_V6_NOT_READY`
