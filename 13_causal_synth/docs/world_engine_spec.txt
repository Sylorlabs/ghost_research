=== WORLD ENGINE SPECIFICATION (Machine-Synthesized) ===

1.  OVERVIEW: The World Engine is a non-deterministic state machine. It exists to provide 'Causal Feedback' to the AGI.

2.  STATE:
    - 8-word State Vector (64-bit each).
    - 8-word Action Vector (64-bit each).
    - Transition Function: P(State_t+1 | State_t, Action_t)

3.  CAUSALITY:
    - The physics are not linear. 
    - Actions are mapped to state bits through a complex mixing function.
    - Noise is injected via a PRNG stream, making deterministic prediction impossible.

4.  GOAL:
    - The AGI must synthesize a Causal Model (a predictive circuit) that minimizes 
      the prediction error between its 'thought' state_t+1 and the 'real' state_t+1.

Truth: Causality is the limit of prediction under noise.
